# 👥 HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing employee turnover, workforce demographics, salary distribution, and departmental attrition patterns using Power BI.

## 📊 Project Overview
This dashboard provides deep human resources insights into overall employee headcount (1,470), total attritions (237), overall attrition rate (16.12%), average employee age (37), average salary ($6.5K), and average tenure (7.0 years) across departments like Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales.

## 🚀 Key Features & Insights
1. **Summary KPIs:** High-level metrics tracking active headcount, total leavers, attrition rate, salary averages, and tenure.
2. **Attrition by Education & Demographics:** Breakdown of leavers by educational background (Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical Degree) and age brackets (led by the 26-35 age group).
3. **Job Role Analysis:** Matrix and bar charts highlighting turnover across roles such as Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives, and Research Scientists.
4. **Compensation & Tenure:** Visualizations tracking attrition across salary slabs (with highest turnover under the 5k slab) and years at the company.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, DAX measures, custom conditional color schemes, and interactive page layouts.
* **Power Query:** Data transformation, cleaning, and preprocessing.
* **GitHub:** Version control and project documentation.
